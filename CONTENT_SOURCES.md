- Phone number and unrelated personal information.
- Private thesis, company or client code.
- Infrastructure addresses, credentials, data copies or production topology.
- Candidate CVs or personal information from recruitment work.
- CERN branding or any suggestion of CERN employment or affiliation.
- Dynamic GitHub counts and fabricated badges.
- Unverified GPU, clinical, industrial, institutional-deployment or distributed-resilience claims.
