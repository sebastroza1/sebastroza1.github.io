# Privacy

This site is static and does not include analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, a contact form, a backend or runtime API tracking.

## Data stored in the browser

The site stores two preferences in `localStorage`:

- selected language;
- selected light or dark theme.

These values stay on the visitor’s device and are not transmitted to the site owner.

## Published personal information

The site publishes professional name, city/country, email, GitHub, LinkedIn and owner-authorized career information. It deliberately excludes the phone number, private certificates, private repositories, production infrastructure details and third-party candidate data.

External links are governed by their destination’s privacy practices. Clicking an email link opens the visitor’s configured mail application.
